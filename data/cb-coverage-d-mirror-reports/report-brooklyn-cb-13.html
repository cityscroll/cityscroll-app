Recommendation

No followable unofficial structured source exists. Facebook and YouTube are unofficial, unstructured, and must not be inventoried. Minutes are already official on nyc.gov and do not need a records request. A future adapter could use the official homepage date list (three tentative 2026 full-board evenings) plus the existing minutes HTML+PDF index — that is official nyc.gov HTML, not an unofficial mirror — with the caveats that calendar.page 404s and must not be inferred, the homepage list is not an ICS, and committee meetings appear only on Facebook. Publishing consistency

  • This is not an empty stub. Staff still update the nyc.gov homepage, minutes PDFs, committee assignment PDFs (21 Aug 2026), Facebook, and YouTube.
  • What they do not publish is a machine calendar. calendar.page was never replaced after it 404’d.
  • Full board is roughly fourth Wednesday at 7 PM (directory + 2026 minutes dates: 28 Jan, 25 Mar, 22 Apr, 20 May all Wednesday evenings). Homepage fall dates skip December and do not list committees.
  • Summer recess is observed (homepage jumps from spring minutes to September dates).
