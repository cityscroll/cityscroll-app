Recommendation

A future adapter could use the official WordPress The Events Calendar REST/ICS at brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us (official, not unofficial). Caveats: restrict to category Upcoming Meetings or titles beginning CB6 so Alternate Side Parking and neighborhood events are not ingested as board meetings; minutes for the current year live on Google Drive (third_party_storage), not a dated HTML PDF index. bkcb6.app is an unofficial civic aggregator and must stay labeled unofficial. Official sources found

brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us is the same class of board-owned WordPress already inventoried for Brooklyn CB7 and CB17. The nyc.gov stub is a leftover pointer, not the working site.

Upcoming meetings — The Events Calendar

board_owned_official Machine paths: REST https://brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us/wp-json/tribe/events/v1/ and category ICS https://brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us/events/category/upcoming-meetings/?ical=1.

Observed full-board meetings (category Upcoming Meetings): 9 Sep, 14 Oct, 11 Nov, 9 Dec 2026, 6:30–8:30pm. Committee meetings (Landmarks/Land Use, Business Affairs, Human Services, Transportation/Parks) are on the main calendar for September–October 2026. FAQ states committees meet on fixed nights in the 3rd/4th weeks at 6:30pm and that the calendar, LINK NYC kiosks, and the District Manager’s weekly newsletter are the posting surfaces.

Caveat: the unfiltered community calendar also contains Alternate Side Parking, Pioneer Works Second Sundays, Old Stone House commemorations, early voting, and Borough President ULURP hearings. An adapter must use the Upcoming Meetings category (or title prefix CB6) or it will ingest neighborhood events as board meetings.

Minutes — HTML year indexes + Google Drive

third_party_storage Current year is Drive (“2026 Meeting Minutes (YTD)”), with yearly HTML pages back to 2000–2009. Fetchable, but Drive folders are not a dated HTML document index with stable per-file URLs on the page. Existing html_pdf_v1 wants explicit record IDs/dates on an HTML index.

Publishing consistency

  • nyc.gov is a husk; the working site is the cityofnewyork.us WordPress. The stub calendar.page at least links there (unlike CB2’s stub, which links nowhere).
  • Full board skips July/August; September 2026 is already scheduled. That matches the FAQ.
  • The “community calendar” is deliberately broader than board meetings. Product ingestion must not treat every Tribe event as a CB meeting.
  • Minutes publication is Drive-first for the current year, HTML archives for closed years.
