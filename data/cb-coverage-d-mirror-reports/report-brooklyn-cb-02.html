Recommendation

A future adapter could use the board-owned Google Calendar ICS bk02public@gmail.com (official, not unofficial). Caveats: the 3.08 MB feed exceeds google_calendar_v1’s 1 MB cap; the calendar mixes BKCB2 meetings with historical FAB events, newsletters, and community happenings and must be filtered by title prefix; The Events Calendar REST on the WordPress site is empty; minutes/agendas sit on Google Drive rather than an HTML index. Facebook / Instagram / YouTube are unofficial mirrors of that calendar and must stay labeled unofficial. No nyc.gov calendar URL should be inferred (the conventional path 404s). This report does not authorize inventory or adapter code changes.

Official sources found (missed by the nyc.gov-stub pass)

These are board-owned official sources on cityofnewyork.us / Google properties the board publishes itself. They are not unofficial mirrors. The 2026-08-13 pass never left the nyc.gov stub, so they were recorded as absent.

Upcoming meetings — Google Calendar bk02public@gmail.com

board_owned_official Format: iCalendar. Fetchable without login.

  • Embed: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=bk02public@gmail.com&ctz=America/New_York
  • ICS: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/bk02public%40gmail.com/public/basic.ics (3.08 MB, 1,006 events)
  • HTML host: https://cbbrooklyn.cityofnewyork.us/cb2/calendar/
  • 2026 full-board VEVENTs titled BKCB2: FULL BOARD MEETING on 21 Jan, 18 Feb, 18 Mar, 22 Apr, 20 May, 17 Jun, 23 Sep, 21 Oct, 18 Nov, 16 Dec (UTC timestamps; local evening).
  • September 2026 committee series is fully populated (Health 1 Sep, Land Use 2 Sep, Transportation 3 Sep, Economic Development 8 Sep, Executive 9 Sep, Parks 10 Sep, Youth 15 Sep, ULURP 240 Nassau 16 Sep, Finance 22 Sep, Full Board 23 Sep).

Adapter fit: existing google_calendar_v1 in site/community_board_source_adapters.mjs. Caveat: that contract caps feeds at 1,000,000 bytes; this ICS is ~3.08 MB and would be rejected until the adapter windows the feed or the cap is raised. The calendar is mixed: 455 BKCB2* events, older “CB2 - …” titles, 142 newsletter placeholders, 36 historical FAB (not this board) events, holidays, and neighborhood happenings. A future adapter must keep only board/committee events (SUMMARY prefix BKCB2: or current CB2 -), not the whole calendar.

Agendas / packets — BKCD2 Public Drive

third_party_storage Google Drive folder published by the board and linked from the official committees page (“Visit the BKCD2 Public Drive”). Folder id 1vP5RRdeO7Hq4skAh3kF3caUOAseeP27w. Not an HTML minutes index. Listing requires Drive HTML/JSON, not a stable per-document HTML page. Airtable/Drive adapters in this repo never invent API URLs from folder links.

Publishing consistency

  • The board has abandoned nyc.gov as a working site (6.5 KB stub) and publishes on the shared Brooklyn WordPress network used by CB7 / CB10 / CB17.
  • Meetings are advertised on a live Google Calendar that already has fall 2026 committee and full-board rows. Summer 2026 has newsletter placeholders rather than meetings (typical July/August recess).
  • Agendas live on Drive, not as dated HTML. Minutes do not have a WordPress or nyc.gov index.
  • Social posts are a human-readable overlay of the same calendar, not an independent dataset.

What a future adapter could use

Official path, with caveats — not an unofficial insertion:

  • Upcoming: Google Calendar ICS bk02public@gmail.com, filtered to BKCB2/CB2 meeting titles, after solving the 1 MB cap.
  • Packets: Drive folder only if a Drive/HTML-index adapter is explicitly registered as third_party_storage.
  • Do not use Tribe REST/ICS (empty). Do not invent nyc.gov calendar/minutes paths. Do not inventory Facebook/Instagram/YouTube.
