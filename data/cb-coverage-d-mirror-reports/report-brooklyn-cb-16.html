Official sources found (path mismatch, not absence)

CB16 is the opposite of “the city does not publish.” Minutes have been posted as PDFs since 2006. The 2026-08-13 pass looked at the wrong URL shape (/minutes/minutes.page instead of /about/board-meeting-minutes.page) and treated a summer-empty calendar as no calendar.

Adapter fit: html_pdf_v1 (explicit HTML index with linked PDFs). One March 2016 file is stored as 03-22-2015.pdf under the 2016 folder — fail closed on filename/year disagreement.

Upcoming — calendar.page, currently blank

nyc_official https://www.nyc.gov/site/brooklyncb16/about/calendar.page

When staffed, this is an HTML table of the current month (work groups + fourth-Tuesday public meeting + Webex passwords). On 2026-08-22 it is honestly empty. An nyc_official_calendar_v1 adapter that requires an explicit heading, publisher date, and page-declared year would correctly yield zero events today and should not be treated as “source missing.” Re-check after Labor Day; June 2026 cache shows the expected shape.

Publishing consistency

  • Homepage is a generic nyc.gov skin; the working documents live one click down under About.
  • Calendar is a monthly overwrite, blank in August. That is a publishing habit, not a missing site.
  • Minutes are the durable record (2006–2026) and are updated through April/May 2026.
  • Hybrid public meetings at Mt. Ollie Baptist Church, 1698 St. Marks Avenue, plus standing Webex MTIDs on the committees page.
  • Fourth Tuesday 7pm cadence holds in 2025–2026 minutes (with December 2025 held virtual-only).
