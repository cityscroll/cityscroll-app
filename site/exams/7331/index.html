What the notice says

Test format: Multiple-choice testing is expected to begin October 22, 2026

Qualifications: A high school diploma or equivalent, plus the license, medical, psychological, and screening requirements in the NOE.

Residency: Under New York City Administrative Code Section 12-120, you might need to be a resident of the City of New York within 90 days of the date you are appointed to this position.

Fee waiver: No application fee is charged for this exam.
