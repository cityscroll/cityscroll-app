Delivery

Create a watch

Follow what you care about. Save a topic, place, agency, or keyword. We email matching public records when they appear.

After 14 quiet days on a daily watch, we send a short still-watching note. Weekly emails are sent on Monday. Edits start with the next digest (about 9am Eastern). Unsubscribing is instant.
