Sources and limits

CityScroll joins City Record agency identity, publisher civil-service certification edges, and auto-certified enacted-law statutory obligations. Deadlines are statutory timed events, not compliance verdicts. Contracts, meetings, and rules come from the entity-intelligence lookup; staffing exams come from publisher certification records; statutory obligations come from independent enacted-law extraction with inspectable source-law links.
