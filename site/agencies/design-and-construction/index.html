How links are warranted

Every connection carries a warrant class. A score or high-confidence band is never the same thing as a confirmed identity — possible links stay possible and are not promoted into verified totals.

  • Exact match — Joined by an exact publisher key or a named identity registry match — not by a similarity score.
  • Probabilistic link — Joined by record-linkage features or a similarity score. A high score is still only a possible link, never a verified identity.
  • Person-accepted — A person accepted or rejected this link after inspecting the evidence. The disposition is the warrant, not an automatic score.
