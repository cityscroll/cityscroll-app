Source anglaise : Explore housing across city records

Source anglaise : Search for housing, compare two kinds of record, and check a result at its official source.

Source anglaise : Last reviewed 2026-09-06

Source anglaise : Step 1 — Search for housing

Source anglaise : Open the CityScroll front page, type housing in the search box and press Rechercher. Search needs JavaScript switched on. No account or email address is needed. The results page shows your word and groups such as Contrats, People + organizations, Foncier, Règles, Réunions, and Examens. Counts vary with the published records.

Source anglaise : Step 2 — Open an agency result

Source anglaise : In People + organizations, select an agency title, such as Housing Preservation and Development when it appears. The agency page names the agency and groups its connected records by kind. If this group is empty, try another agency-related word in the search field.

Source anglaise : Step 3 — Compare a rule result

Source anglaise : Use your browser's Back button to return to the housing search. In Règles, read a result's match explanation. A passage match quotes the published sentence containing your topic. Rules include proposals and final rules: check the record's stage before treating it as an open chance to comment. A final rule is not a proposal.

Source anglaise : Step 4 — Check the official copy

Source anglaise : For a result with a quoted passage, open Source officielle beside that passage. Check the title, agency and stage on the city's published copy. If no passage result appears, open a result's title and use its Voir dans le Registre municipal link instead. You now have a source you can rely on, rather than just a search match.

Source anglaise : Step 5 — Keep and repeat the search

Source anglaise : Return to the search results and copy the browser address. Paste it into a new tab: housing and the grouped view should return, although the records can change. Repeat with a street or agency you care about. Compare two groups again and check one official copy. The glossary explains unfamiliar record types without being a prerequisite.

Source anglaise : When a source cannot load

Source anglaise : A failed source is unknown, not a group with no records. Reload the search or use the record's official link. An empty group alone does not establish that nothing happened.
