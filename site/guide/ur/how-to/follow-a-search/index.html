انگریزی ماخذ: Follow a search and manage your updates

انگریزی ماخذ: Preview a watch, create it with your email address, and manage or stop its updates.

انگریزی ماخذ: Last reviewed 2026-09-10

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 1 — Set the topic and place

انگریزی ماخذ: Open الرٹس with JavaScript switched on. Under آپ کس چیز کی پیروی کرنا چاہتے ہیں؟, choose a topic such as Contracts and RFPs. Under Where?, choose a borough or Any place. Open نتائج محدود کریں to add a keyword or agency. For contracts, open زیادہ درست میل to include any or all of a few words, treat an entry as an exact phrase, or exclude words. Example: include software, exclude maintenance, preview the awards, then tighten the exclusion if a useful record such as a Greenway project was removed because its body mentioned maintenance vehicles. Frozen award examples are published awards, not currently open bids. The selected choices describe an unsaved watch. No account or password is needed. This is separate from the city's own account system.

انگریزی ماخذ: Public meetings use the same زیادہ درست میل control. The board, place, and upcoming-event window you already chose stay the boundary. Extra wording only narrows it. Exact Council-matter watches and other topics do not offer these controls.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 2 — Check the preview

انگریزی ماخذ: Press مطابقت رکھنے والے نتائج کا پیش منظر below the choices. Read the rule sentence in پیروی کا خلاصہ, then check پیروی کی شرائط and the records under پیش نظارہ. A preview is not a subscription: nothing is saved and no email is sent. Change a choice and press مطابقت رکھنے والے نتائج تازہ کریں to revise it.

انگریزی ماخذ: The message No matches now — still watch for new means the loaded preview is empty. You can save a watch for future records, or widen a criterion and preview again. If the preview is not ready, use its retry control. The current matches are unknown.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 3 — Create the watch

انگریزی ماخذ: Under ای میل کی تعداد, choose روزانہ (on days with matches) or ہفتہ وار (Monday digest). Under یہ پیروی بنائیں, enter ای میل پتہ and press پیروی بنائیں. The watch exists as soon as the request succeeds. There is no confirmation email to click.

انگریزی ماخذ: Check for آپ کی رکنیت ہو گئی ہے — ہم آپ کو ای میل بھیجیں گے۔ کسی بھی وقت رکنیت کا انتظام یا اسے منسوخ کریں۔ Keep the welcome email's manage link. If the page reports an error, use its retry instruction. An error does not confirm enrollment.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 4 — Change or stop updates

انگریزی ماخذ: Open the manage link in a CityScroll email. On the watch's row, change the frequency and press Save, or choose Pause, Unpause, or Delete watch. Check the row's resulting state. Edits apply to the next digest, around 9am Eastern. Unsubscribe all watches stops all updates immediately, so use it only when that is what you want.

انگریزی ماخذ: When saved watches are unavailable

انگریزی ماخذ: Under آپ کی پیرویاں, an unrecognized browser asks you to open a CityScroll email. Use that email's manage link. A link reported as invalid or expired needs replacing with the link in a recent email. If saved watches cannot load, use the offered retry. That failure does not mean they were deleted. A watch link reported as unrecognized has saved nothing: choose a topic on Following and start again.
