انگریزی ماخذ: Put dates in your calendar

انگریزی ماخذ: Add a single event or subscribe to a dated list, then check the result in your calendar app.

انگریزی ماخذ: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 1 — Choose the dates

انگریزی ماخذ: Open ابھی with JavaScript switched on. No account or email address is needed. اس تاریخ تک عمل کریں lists action deadlines. جلد ہونے والا lists events. Read the row's date and open its title to check the published record before saving it.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 2 — Save one event

انگریزی ماخذ: Open اجلاس and select a meeting title to open its meeting page.

انگریزی ماخذ: On a meeting's page, choose Add to calendar among its actions. This control appears only when the record has a clock time. Open the downloaded file in your calendar app and check the event's title, date and time.

انگریزی ماخذ: This is a one-time copy: it will not update if the event changes. If only a day is published, the control is absent. CityScroll supplies no clock time for a date-only event. For a continuing list, use the next step.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 3 — Open a calendar subscription

انگریزی ماخذ: Open اجلاس. Enter a topic in تلاش. Open مزید فلٹرز to choose تاریخ کی مدت or متاثرہ علاقہ. Then choose کیلنڈر کی رکنیت لیں in the toolbar above the records. The control appears only when the list contains dated items. If absent, widen the filters until dated records appear. The panel names the list you are about to subscribe to.

انگریزی ماخذ: If the filter panel covers the toolbar, close مزید فلٹرز and choose کیلنڈر کی رکنیت لیں.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 4 — Add the subscription in your calendar app

انگریزی ماخذ: In that panel, choose the route your app supports:

  • انگریزی ماخذ: کیلنڈر کی رکنیت کھولیں opens your device's calendar app. Add the subscription there. In Apple Calendar, set its Auto-refresh frequency.
  • انگریزی ماخذ: سبسکرپشن URL نقل کریں copies an address. In Google Calendar, paste it under Other calendars → From URL. In Outlook, use Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

انگریزی ماخذ: Check in your calendar app that it lists a subscribed calendar and displays the expected dates. CityScroll cannot confirm that a subscription was added or refreshed. Importing a downloaded file is a one-time copy. Only a URL your calendar keeps fetching is a subscription.

انگریزی ماخذ: Subscriptions keep date-only deadlines as all-day entries and mark cancelled events as cancelled. Updates follow your calendar app's refresh schedule. Check the official record for last-minute changes.

انگریزی ماخذ: When dates are missing

انگریزی ماخذ: If Now lists an action with no published closing date, there is no deadline to export. Open الرٹس to watch for updates instead. If a source could not be reached, the answer is unknown: reload before treating the list as empty.
