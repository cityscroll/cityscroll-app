انگریزی ماخذ: Find and narrow records

انگریزی ماخذ: Find a topic, narrow the relevant collection, and keep a link to its filters.

انگریزی ماخذ: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 1 — Search for your topic

انگریزی ماخذ: Open تلاش, type a word such as housing in the search field and press تلاش. This needs JavaScript switched on, but no account or email address. Results are grouped by kind of record. Read each result's match explanation before choosing it. Counts change as records change.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 2 — Open the relevant collection

انگریزی ماخذ: Open Browse and choose the kind of record you need. Use People + organizations for agencies, boards and vendors, عملہ for appointments and hiring outcomes, or امتحانات for civil-service applications. The destination's heading confirms the collection.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 3 — Narrow the list

انگریزی ماخذ: For a dated example, open اجلاس. In the filters above the results, enter a word in تلاش. Open مزید فلٹرز to choose تاریخ کی مدت, متاثرہ علاقہ and ایجنسی. The list updates to match. Other collections offer different controls. Controls and what they give you is an optional lookup.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 4 — Keep and reopen the view

انگریزی ماخذ: Copy the full address from your browser's address bar and paste it into a new tab. Check that the collection and selected filters match. The link repeats your query against the records available when opened. It does not freeze today's results.

انگریزی ماخذ: Step 5 — Check the record's current opportunity

انگریزی ماخذ: Open a result by its title and read its status, dates and official-source link. For practice, Caseworker, exam 7016 has a بند application window. That historical record teaches the process. It is not an invitation to apply. For a current application, return to امتحانات and check an open window at its official source.

انگریزی ماخذ: When results are empty or unknown

انگریزی ماخذ: Remove a filter or shorten the keyword in the same controls, then search again. A loaded list with no matches describes the records searched, not everything the city has done. If a source could not be reached, its answer is unknown. Reload or open the record's official source before concluding it has no records.
