영어 원문: Where the records come from

영어 원문: Which publishers CityScroll reads, how current their records are, and what falls outside what it can show you.

영어 원문: Last reviewed 2026-09-06

영어 원문: Everything here was published by someone else

영어 원문: CityScroll holds no records of its own. Every record on it was published by a New York City agency, a state body, or another official publisher, and every record keeps a link back to that published copy. CityScroll is independent of city government and speaks for no agency.

영어 원문: The largest single source is The City Record, the city's own daily publication of official notices, which is also released as open data. Alongside it sit the city's contract, budget, payroll, buildings, planning, civil-service and property datasets, and a few state publishers.

영어 원문: How often the sources change

영어 원문: Some publishers release as things happen, and some release on a cycle. That difference is the commonest reason something the city has already done has not reached this site yet, so it is worth knowing which kind of source you are reading.

영어 원문: What coverage means

영어 원문: Coverage is not one number, and CityScroll does not offer one. It varies by source, and there are four different ways a source can be working perfectly and still not answer your question.

영어 원문: The case영어 원문: What it means for what you see
영어 원문: The source is complete but narrow영어 원문: The City Record covers what the city publishes in The City Record. Things the city does elsewhere are not in it
영어 원문: The source is read but not joined영어 원문: The record is here and the record that would connect it to what you are reading is not, so the connection is absent although both ends exist
영어 원문: The source is closed영어 원문: The publisher has stopped adding to it. It stays because it is the record of its period
영어 원문: The source is paused영어 원문: It is not currently being read. It stays listed rather than quietly disappearing, so a stopped feed cannot be mistaken for a subject with nothing in it

영어 원문: What is not covered

  • 영어 원문: Anything the city has not published. Where a decision was made and no public record of it was released, you will not find it here. That absence tells you about publication and nothing about the decision.
  • 영어 원문: Anything published in a form that cannot be joined. A document released only as a scan, or with no identifier tying it to anything else, may be published and still unreachable from a record you are reading.
  • 영어 원문: Anything private. Records about identifiable individuals — exam scores, applicant details — are not published by the city as open data and are not here.

영어 원문: When a source is having a bad day

영어 원문: A source that could not be reached is a different thing from a source with nothing to say, and the site keeps them apart. A page that could not load its records says so, and retrying or opening the official source is the way through. Reading a temporary failure as an absence of records is the mistake this distinction exists to prevent.

영어 원문: The same goes the other way. A record set that is genuinely empty says it is empty, rather than showing an error and leaving you to guess.
