영어 원문: Dates and calendars

컨트롤영어 원문: What it does영어 원문: What you are left with
마감 전 행동영어 원문: Separates deadlines you can still act on from the events that follow them영어 원문: A reading of which dates are yours to meet
캘린더 구독영어 원문: Offers a subscription for what you are looking at영어 원문: A panel with two ways to take it into your own calendar
캘린더 구독 열기영어 원문: Hands the feed to the calendar your device has영어 원문: Your calendar app takes over
구독 URL 복사영어 원문: Copies the address to paste into a calendar that asks for one영어 원문: An address on your clipboard

영어 원문: Two limits are worth knowing. 캘린더 구독 appears only where what you are looking at has dated events a calendar could hold. On a listing with no such dates it is not offered, so no empty feed is offered. And once the address has reached your calendar app, CityScroll cannot tell whether your calendar kept the subscription or how often it refreshes — that is a setting in your calendar, and some of them take hours to show a change.
