영어 원문: Check the evidence behind a connection

영어 원문: Inspect a connection and copy a link that reopens its evidence.

영어 원문: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

영어 원문: Step 1 — Open a connection's details

영어 원문: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. Under 연결된 기록, choose 상세 정보 beside a record's title (announced as 상세 정보 by a screen reader). A 연결 근거 panel opens for that record.

영어 원문: For the worked example, use 상세 정보 beside Update to Parks List of Qualifying Documents for Disability Membership Fee, if present. The example connection link opens that panel directly when the record is available.

영어 원문: Step 2 — Read the basis and source

영어 원문: In 연결 근거, read the match label and date. In the example, 공개된 기록으로 일치 확인 and Matched to the agency's published name describe the basis. Under 이 연결이 생성된 방법, read the relation, issued rule. Open City Record 공고 in the panel to compare the official source, then return.

영어 원문: This supports the stated connection, not a finding of compliance or wrongdoing. How records are connected explains the other match labels.

영어 원문: Step 3 — Reopen the evidence from its link

영어 원문: At the bottom of the panel, choose 이 연결의 링크 복사. Copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that the same record's 연결 근거 is expanded. Keep this address with the claim it supports.

영어 원문: When there is no source or panel

영어 원문: Some contract connections have no separate notice source. Open the linked record title in the panel and use that record's own source link. Missing source evidence is not proof of no relationship. If a copied address opens no panel, return to 연결된 기록 and use 상세 정보 to select a connection currently on the page. Without JavaScript, read the records directly. The panel is unavailable.
