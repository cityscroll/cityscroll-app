영어 원문: Find and narrow records

영어 원문: Find a topic, narrow the relevant collection, and keep a link to its filters.

영어 원문: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

영어 원문: Step 1 — Search for your topic

영어 원문: Open 검색, type a word such as housing in the search field and press 검색. This needs JavaScript switched on, but no account or email address. Results are grouped by kind of record. Read each result's match explanation before choosing it. Counts change as records change.

영어 원문: Step 2 — Open the relevant collection

영어 원문: Open Browse and choose the kind of record you need. Use People + organizations for agencies, boards and vendors, 인력 for appointments and hiring outcomes, or 시험 for civil-service applications. The destination's heading confirms the collection.

영어 원문: Step 3 — Narrow the list

영어 원문: For a dated example, open 회의. In the filters above the results, enter a word in 검색. Open 추가 필터 to choose 날짜 범위, 영향 지역 and 기관. The list updates to match. Other collections offer different controls. Controls and what they give you is an optional lookup.

영어 원문: Step 4 — Keep and reopen the view

영어 원문: Copy the full address from your browser's address bar and paste it into a new tab. Check that the collection and selected filters match. The link repeats your query against the records available when opened. It does not freeze today's results.

영어 원문: Step 5 — Check the record's current opportunity

영어 원문: Open a result by its title and read its status, dates and official-source link. For practice, Caseworker, exam 7016 has a 마감됨 application window. That historical record teaches the process. It is not an invitation to apply. For a current application, return to 시험 and check an open window at its official source.

영어 원문: When results are empty or unknown

영어 원문: Remove a filter or shorten the keyword in the same controls, then search again. A loaded list with no matches describes the records searched, not everything the city has done. If a source could not be reached, its answer is unknown. Reload or open the record's official source before concluding it has no records.
