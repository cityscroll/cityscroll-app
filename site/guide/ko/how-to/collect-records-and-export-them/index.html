영어 원문: Step 1 — Pin two records

영어 원문: Open the West 50th Street award notice with JavaScript switched on. Among the record's actions, choose 고정. The confirmation links to the workspace and shows the item count. Open the named organization and choose 고정 there too. Pin appears on individual records, not search results.

영어 원문: Step 2 — Add and check a note

영어 원문: Open 내 조사 in the page footer, or your investigation. Check that both records appear under 조사 워크스페이스 · 이 브라우저에만 저장됨. No account is needed. Under a record, type in 메모 추가…, then click outside the field to save. Reload and check that the note remains.

영어 원문: In an ordinary signed-out visit, the collection stays in this browser. Clearing its site data removes it. A session recognized through a CityScroll email can keep pins across devices.

영어 원문: Step 3 — Export a copy

영어 원문: Below the items, choose .csv로 내보내기 for a spreadsheet or .json으로 내보내기 for structured data. Open the downloaded file and check the records, notes and links. Both files are fixed copies. They do not update. 조사 자료 인쇄 opens the browser's print window to print or save a PDF.

영어 원문: Step 4 — Share only after checking the notes

영어 원문: Read your notes before choosing 공유(읽기 전용 링크) below the items. This uploads a snapshot, including notes, to CityScroll. Anyone with the link can read it without a password for 90 days. It is separate from your local list and does not follow later edits.

영어 원문: Open the returned link in a new tab. Shared investigation should show the records and notes without editing controls. A recipient can import a copy into their own investigation.

영어 원문: When an action is unavailable

영어 원문: If sharing reports a server failure, keep the local list and use .csv로 내보내기 or .json으로 내보내기. 연구 패키지 고정 requires a supported comparison. For ordinary pinned records use those exports.

영어 원문: Choosing 전체 삭제 empties the local investigation immediately without confirmation. Use it only when ready to remove that list. Existing exported files and shared snapshots remain. An empty workspace explains how to start again: open an individual record and choose 고정.
