Английский источник: Read a land-use project's next step and documents

Английский источник: Check a project’s current stage, next published opportunity and official documents.

Английский источник: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Английский источник: Step 1 — Open the project

Английский источник: Open land-use projects with JavaScript switched on. Enter the project's name in Индекс, адрес или район above the list. For practice use Timbale Terrace. Select its title and check project number 2022M0258. Reading here submits nothing and starts no subscription.

Английский источник: Step 2 — Read the stage and authority

Английский источник: In Текущее положение, read Текущий этап, Ответственная сторона and Должность together. The role tells you whether the body can decide or only recommend. A recommendation is not approval. Then read Ожидаемый следующий этап and Следующая опубликованная возможность. The former is the usual next step, while the latter is a published chance to participate when a date is available.

Английский источник: Check each date's label before planning around it. A window calculated from statutory review periods is not an appointment. Clocks can pause. Use a published opportunity's official source to confirm where and when to participate.

Английский источник: Step 3 — Open the filed documents

Английский источник: On the project page, find Документы о решении and open a document title if one is listed. These are a body's published decisions. Application filings, including a Racial Equity Report where required, serve a different purpose. Read whether a report was required and observed before drawing a conclusion from its absence.

Английский источник: Use Открыть полный проект ZAP in the decision-documents area to open its full record in the city's Zoning Application Portal. Check that the project number matches and open the filed document there. If no document is listed on CityScroll, use that same portal record to check the released package.

Английский источник: When a stage or date is unknown

Английский источник: An unknown Текущее положение means the available evidence does not establish the stage. No published next opportunity found does not mean there will be none. A source not checked or marked stale leaves the answer unknown. Check the project's portal record and the relevant body's published calendar before making plans.

Английский источник: A report that has not been observed is not automatically late or missing from the applicant's filing. Keep that distinct from a publisher's explicit finding that it was not filed on time.
