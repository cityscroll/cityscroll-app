Английский источник: Look at records as of a day

Английский источник: Filter an agency to a chosen date and share or clear that view.

Английский источник: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Английский источник: Step 1 — Apply a day

Английский источник: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. In the По состоянию на день panel, enter 1 June 2024 in По состоянию на and press Применить. The date stays selected and the summary counts records dated on or before it. The Parks example link opens the same filtered view.

Английский источник: This filters currently linked records by their event or publication date. It is not a reconstruction of what CityScroll knew that day. Undated records are excluded because they cannot be placed on a timeline.

Английский источник: Step 2 — Inspect what was set aside

Английский источник: Under the count, open Более поздние записи. These records have dates after your chosen day. Compare the retained and later counts. Their values can change as records are added or corrected. An empty retained set means no dated records currently linked here meet the cutoff, not that the agency did nothing then.

Английский источник: Step 3 — Share the selected day

Английский источник: While the date is applied, copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that По состоянию на holds the same day and the summary still names the cutoff. Share that address to repeat the filter against the records available when opened.

Английский источник: Step 4 — Clear the filter

Английский источник: Next to Применить, choose Очистить, which appears after a date is set. The date selection clears and the page again invites you to pick a day. Repeat these steps on another agency from People + organizations when you need a different cutoff.

Английский источник: When the filter does not apply

Английский источник: Without JavaScript, submitting the date returns an unfiltered page. Enable JavaScript and press Применить again, then check the summary before relying on the result.
