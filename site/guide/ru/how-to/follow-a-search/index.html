Английский источник: Preview a watch, create it with your email address, and manage or stop its updates.

Английский источник: Last reviewed 2026-09-10

Английский источник: Step 1 — Set the topic and place

Английский источник: Open Оповещения with JavaScript switched on. Under За чем вы хотите следить?, choose a topic such as Contracts and RFPs. Under Where?, choose a borough or Any place. Open Уточнить поиск to add a keyword or agency. For contracts, open Уточнить совпадения to include any or all of a few words, treat an entry as an exact phrase, or exclude words. Example: include software, exclude maintenance, preview the awards, then tighten the exclusion if a useful record such as a Greenway project was removed because its body mentioned maintenance vehicles. Frozen award examples are published awards, not currently open bids. The selected choices describe an unsaved watch. No account or password is needed. This is separate from the city's own account system.

Английский источник: Public meetings use the same Уточнить совпадения control. The board, place, and upcoming-event window you already chose stay the boundary. Extra wording only narrows it. Exact Council-matter watches and other topics do not offer these controls.

Английский источник: Step 2 — Check the preview

Английский источник: Press Предпросмотр совпадений below the choices. Read the rule sentence in Сводка отслеживания, then check Условия отслеживания and the records under Предпросмотр. A preview is not a subscription: nothing is saved and no email is sent. Change a choice and press Обновить совпадения to revise it.

Английский источник: The message No matches now — still watch for new means the loaded preview is empty. You can save a watch for future records, or widen a criterion and preview again. If the preview is not ready, use its retry control. The current matches are unknown.

Английский источник: Step 3 — Create the watch

Английский источник: Under Частота писем, choose Ежедневно (on days with matches) or Еженедельно (Monday digest). Under Создать это отслеживание, enter Адрес электронной почты and press Создать отслеживание. The watch exists as soon as the request succeeds. There is no confirmation email to click.

Английский источник: Check for Вы подписаны — мы будем присылать письма. Изменить или отменить подписку можно в любое время. Keep the welcome email's manage link. If the page reports an error, use its retry instruction. An error does not confirm enrollment.

Английский источник: Step 4 — Change or stop updates

Английский источник: Open the manage link in a CityScroll email. On the watch's row, change the frequency and press Save, or choose Pause, Unpause, or Delete watch. Check the row's resulting state. Edits apply to the next digest, around 9am Eastern. Unsubscribe all watches stops all updates immediately, so use it only when that is what you want.

Английский источник: When saved watches are unavailable

Английский источник: Under Ваши отслеживания, an unrecognized browser asks you to open a CityScroll email. Use that email's manage link. A link reported as invalid or expired needs replacing with the link in a recent email. If saved watches cannot load, use the offered retry. That failure does not mean they were deleted. A watch link reported as unrecognized has saved nothing: choose a topic on Following and start again.
