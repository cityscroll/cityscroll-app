Английский источник: Trace a notice to the duty behind it

Английский источник: Follow a Sanitation rule notice to its legal duty, source law and publication evidence.

Английский источник: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Английский источник: Step 1 — Open the notice

Английский источник: Open the Sanitation final rule notice. No account is needed, and the notice and duty pages read without JavaScript. Check the title: DSNY Final Rule re Implementation Dates for the Brooklyn North and Upper Manhattan Commercial Waste Zones. Open Открыть в Городском вестнике beside the notice's actions to compare the city's copy, then return.

Английский источник: Step 2 — Follow the duty

Английский источник: On the notice, find Connected mandate. Read Rules filing for this duty, the relation, and New York City Charter § 753(e)(2), the citation. Select the duty text about regulating commercial waste businesses. It opens the Sanitation duty, with its agency, required action and citation.

Английский источник: Step 3 — Check the law

Английский источник: On the duty page, open Source law. The underlying legislation opens at its official source. Compare the citation and required action, then return to the duty page.

Английский источник: Step 4 — Close the loop

Английский источник: Under Publication evidence, find the notice's request number, 20260605008. It links the filing you started from back to this duty. This establishes that the filing was published. A compliance finding would also need to establish whether the filing met the duty. This page makes no such finding.

Английский источник: Step 5 — Try another notice

Английский источник: Open another rule notice from Правила, find Connected mandate, then follow its duty text and Source law. If the block is absent, no supported connection is shown. That does not mean no law applies. Check the notice's own official copy instead.

Английский источник: Optional — Watch for later filings

Английский источник: On a notice with Connected mandate, select Watch this mandate beside the connection. Following opens with the duty selected and needs JavaScript switched on. Check Предпросмотр совпадений there. Previewing does not subscribe you. Choose a frequency, enter Адрес электронной почты under Создать это отслеживание, and press Создать отслеживание only if you want email updates. The page's subscribed confirmation is the saved result.
