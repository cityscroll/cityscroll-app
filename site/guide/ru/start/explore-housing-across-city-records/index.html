Английский источник: Explore housing across city records

Английский источник: Search for housing, compare two kinds of record, and check a result at its official source.

Английский источник: Last reviewed 2026-09-06

Английский источник: Step 1 — Search for housing

Английский источник: Open the CityScroll front page, type housing in the search box and press Поиск. Search needs JavaScript switched on. No account or email address is needed. The results page shows your word and groups such as Контракты, People + organizations, Земля, Правила, Заседания, and Экзамены. Counts vary with the published records.

Английский источник: Step 2 — Open an agency result

Английский источник: In People + organizations, select an agency title, such as Housing Preservation and Development when it appears. The agency page names the agency and groups its connected records by kind. If this group is empty, try another agency-related word in the search field.

Английский источник: Step 3 — Compare a rule result

Английский источник: Use your browser's Back button to return to the housing search. In Правила, read a result's match explanation. A passage match quotes the published sentence containing your topic. Rules include proposals and final rules: check the record's stage before treating it as an open chance to comment. A final rule is not a proposal.

Английский источник: Step 4 — Check the official copy

Английский источник: For a result with a quoted passage, open Официальный источник beside that passage. Check the title, agency and stage on the city's published copy. If no passage result appears, open a result's title and use its Открыть в Городском вестнике link instead. You now have a source you can rely on, rather than just a search match.

Английский источник: Step 5 — Keep and repeat the search

Английский источник: Return to the search results and copy the browser address. Paste it into a new tab: housing and the grouped view should return, although the records can change. Repeat with a street or agency you care about. Compare two groups again and check one official copy. The glossary explains unfamiliar record types without being a prerequisite.

Английский источник: When a source cannot load

Английский источник: A failed source is unknown, not a group with no records. Reload the search or use the record's official link. An empty group alone does not establish that nothing happened.
