Sous anglè: Dates and calendars

KontwòlSous anglè: What it doesSous anglè: What you are left with
Aji anvanSous anglè: Separates deadlines you can still act on from the events that follow themSous anglè: A reading of which dates are yours to meet
Abòne ak kalandriye aSous anglè: Offers a subscription for what you are looking atSous anglè: A panel with two ways to take it into your own calendar
Louvri abònman kalandriye aSous anglè: Hands the feed to the calendar your device hasSous anglè: Your calendar app takes over
Kopye URL abònman anSous anglè: Copies the address to paste into a calendar that asks for oneSous anglè: An address on your clipboard

Sous anglè: Two limits are worth knowing. Abòne ak kalandriye a appears only where what you are looking at has dated events a calendar could hold. On a listing with no such dates it is not offered, so no empty feed is offered. And once the address has reached your calendar app, CityScroll cannot tell whether your calendar kept the subscription or how often it refreshes — that is a setting in your calendar, and some of them take hours to show a change.
