Sous anglè: Observe City Government

Sous anglè: Compare six public government experiences, then follow the official access step for the one you want to see.

Dènye revizyon: 2026-11-25

English source: Step 1 — Choose an observation experience

Sous anglè: These are six different experiences, not six versions of the same meeting. Read the preparation note before opening a calendar or registration page. A dated CityScroll detail page is a way back to the matching guide section; it does not change the access rules of the official publisher.

Sous anglè: Watch a PDC public review

Sa ou pral wè: yon reyinyon Public Design Commission, abitye yon reyinyon komite nan maten ki vin apre yon reyinyon piblik ak eleman konsantman ak nenpòt odyans piblik. Obsève se separe de pale.

Aksè: patisipe nan Public Design Commission Board Room la, nan twazyèm etaj City Hall, konekte pa Microsoft Teams, oswa gade dirèk YouTube la. Ou ka obsève san ou pa enskri pou temwaye. Enskripsyon pou temwaye sou Teams, lè yo ofri li, se yon aksyon separe epi li pa obligatwa sèlman pou obsève.

Sous anglè: Prepare: Open the agenda and confirm the date, time, meeting format, and any published materials. Keep the official page open in case the agency changes the details.

Pwochen etap ofisyèl: li enfòmasyon reyinyon Public Design Commission lan. Pou yon eleman pwograme, chwazi Louvri detay pwograme sou kat li nan Obsève Gouvènman Vil la.

Sous anglè: Listen to a BSA executive review

Sous anglè: What you see: A Board of Standards and Appeals executive review of applications and cases. This is listen-only for an observer; the executive review is not a public testimony signup.

Aksè: sèvi ak paj enfòmasyon odyans k ap vini yo nan BSA; yo tou transmèt odyans yo an dirèk sou YouTube. Obsève pa vle di ou enskri kòm moun k ap soumèt òf oswa moun k ap pale.

Sous anglè: Prepare: Read the agenda, case numbers, and any attached hearing material. Do not treat a prior case date in a case history as another upcoming session.

Pwochen etap ofisyèl: louvri enfòmasyon odyans k ap vini yo nan BSA. Odyans BSA pwograme yo nan lis nan Obsève Gouvènman Vil la.

Sous anglè: Request an OATH trial observation

Sous anglè: What you see: An administrative trial listed in OATH's current calendar. Some trials are remote and some are in person, and the calendar does not confirm that a trial will proceed.

Sous anglè: Access: Ask OATH's calendar unit for observation access. Email OATHCalUnit@OATH.nyc.gov with the calendar index, date, and time. Copying the generated request is not sending it and does not promise admission.

Sous anglè: Prepare: Select a current row marked for trial, then copy its index, date, and start time exactly. Ask the calendar unit to confirm whether it is proceeding and to provide the location or remote instructions. A conference row is not a trial row.

Sous anglè: Official next step: Open OATH's trial calendar. Use the trial-observation request guide for the email checklist.

Sous anglè: Visit a CCRB public trial

Sous anglè: What you see: A public Civilian Complaint Review Board disciplinary trial at 1 Police Plaza. The officer, borough, and charges in the rolling grid are case information; charges are allegations, not findings.

Aksè: sèvi ak kalandriye ofisyèl aktyèl pwosè CCRB. Se sous dat ki disponib yo; CCRB pibliye pwosè kat mwa davans, epi dat yo ka chanje.

Sous anglè: Prepare: Check the grid immediately before you go. It does not provide a stable case identifier or a session start time in every row, so do not copy an old date list into a CityScroll event or infer a venue from the incident borough.

Sous anglè: Official next step: Open the current CCRB trial grid. A scheduled detail can return to the CCRB guide section.

Sous anglè: Enter a Hart Island public-visit program

Sous anglè: What you see: A Ranger-led public visit to Hart Island. General public visits are different from close-person gravesite visits; you do not need a personal connection for the general program.

Sous anglè: Access: Apply through the official Parks registration program for a selected visit date. If demand exceeds capacity, the program uses a lottery. A selected-Tuesday policy is not a weekly event schedule.

Prepare: li règ vizit yo, ansanm ak nenpòt avi ki di vizit yo ranvwaye, epi tann pwogram enskripsyon an pibliye dat. Pa sipoze disponiblite, yon evènman ki repete chak madi, ni yon kote konfime nan gid sa a.

Pwochen etap ofisyèl: Li enfòmasyon sou vizit Hart Island yo. Enstriksyon enskripsyon ki sou paj la se otorite a pou dat yo chwazi ak kapasite a.

Sous anglè: Watch a DDC bid opening

Sous anglè: What you see: A Department of Design and Construction construction bid opening, where submitted prices may become public. A price read at an opening is not a contract award.

Sous anglè: Access: Use the official DDC construction contracts page and its live bid-opening or results links. Watching an opening is separate from becoming a bidder and does not require bidder registration.

Sous anglè: Prepare: Read the agency's instructions and distinguish a live opening from preliminary bid results. This guide does not invent a recurring event, confirmed availability, solicitation identity, or registration requirement.

Sous anglè: Official next step: Open DDC construction contracts or read preliminary bid results.

English source: Keep the official page in view

Sous anglè: The official agency page controls the current access details. CityScroll can explain the experience and connect a scheduled record back to this guide, but an undated program is not an upcoming event and a calendar row is not proof that a proceeding will happen.
