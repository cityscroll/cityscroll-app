Sous anglè: Look at records as of a day

Sous anglè: Filter an agency to a chosen date and share or clear that view.

Sous anglè: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Sous anglè: Step 1 — Apply a day

Sous anglè: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. In the Jiska dat la panel, enter 1 June 2024 in Nan dat and press Aplike. The date stays selected and the summary counts records dated on or before it. The Parks example link opens the same filtered view.

Sous anglè: This filters currently linked records by their event or publication date. It is not a reconstruction of what CityScroll knew that day. Undated records are excluded because they cannot be placed on a timeline.

Sous anglè: Step 2 — Inspect what was set aside

Sous anglè: Under the count, open Dosye apre dat la. These records have dates after your chosen day. Compare the retained and later counts. Their values can change as records are added or corrected. An empty retained set means no dated records currently linked here meet the cutoff, not that the agency did nothing then.

Sous anglè: Step 3 — Share the selected day

Sous anglè: While the date is applied, copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that Nan dat holds the same day and the summary still names the cutoff. Share that address to repeat the filter against the records available when opened.

Sous anglè: Step 4 — Clear the filter

Sous anglè: Next to Aplike, choose Efase, which appears after a date is set. The date selection clears and the page again invites you to pick a day. Repeat these steps on another agency from People + organizations when you need a different cutoff.

Sous anglè: When the filter does not apply

Sous anglè: Without JavaScript, submitting the date returns an unfiltered page. Enable JavaScript and press Aplike again, then check the summary before relying on the result.
