Follow a Community Board

Sous anglè: Create an email watch for one Community Board and keep the link that manages it.

Sous anglè: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Sous anglè: Step 1 — Open the board picker

Sous anglè: Open Alèt. Under Kisa ou vle swiv?, choose Seyans piblik ak reyinyon, then open Limite rezilta yo. The Konsèy Kominotè group appears for this topic. Use a browser with JavaScript switched on. You need an email address only when saving.

Sous anglè: Step 2 — Choose the borough and board

Sous anglè: In Konsèy Kominotè, set Awondisman to Manhattan and Nimewo konsèy la to 7. Both fields are required to identify Manhattan Community Board 7.

Sous anglè: A Community Board is numbered within its borough. City Council District 7 is a different area. Its watch is under More topics → City Council District weekly, not this picker.

Sous anglè: Step 3 — Preview the watch

Sous anglè: Press Gade rezilta ki koresponn yo below the choices. Check that Rezime swivi a and Kritè swivi a both name Manhattan Community Board 7. Previewing saves nothing and sends no email. After changing a choice, press Mete rezilta ki koresponn yo ajou to check it again.

Sous anglè: The preview shows matching board meetings. Gade rezilta aktyèl ki koresponn yo opens a wider district view that can include other bodies' meetings. Use the preview to check what the watch would email. An empty preview does not prove the board has no meetings. Check its calendar through Community Boards if a meeting you expected is missing.

Sous anglè: Step 4 — Save and check the result

Sous anglè: Under Konbyen fwa pou voye imèl, choose Chak jou or Chak semèn. Under Kreye swivi sa a, fill in Adrès imèl, then press Kreye swivi. This starts the subscription immediately. There is no activation link to click.

Sous anglè: Wait for Ou abòne — n ap voye imèl ba ou. Ou ka jere oswa anile abònman an nenpòt lè. Keep the welcome email: its manage link opens the saved watch, where you can change or stop it. A failed submission is not a saved watch. Follow the error's retry instruction.

Sous anglè: When the preview cannot load

Sous anglè: If the preview says it is not ready, use its retry control. That answer is unknown, unlike a loaded preview with no matches. You can still read the board's own published calendar.
