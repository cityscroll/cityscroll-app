Sous anglè: Put dates in your calendar

Sous anglè: Add a single event or subscribe to a dated list, then check the result in your calendar app.

Sous anglè: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Sous anglè: Step 1 — Choose the dates

Sous anglè: Open Kounye a with JavaScript switched on. No account or email address is needed. Aji anvan lists action deadlines. K ap fèt talè lists events. Read the row's date and open its title to check the published record before saving it.

Sous anglè: Step 2 — Save one event

Sous anglè: Open Reyinyon and select a meeting title to open its meeting page.

Sous anglè: On a meeting's page, choose Add to calendar among its actions. This control appears only when the record has a clock time. Open the downloaded file in your calendar app and check the event's title, date and time.

Sous anglè: This is a one-time copy: it will not update if the event changes. If only a day is published, the control is absent. CityScroll supplies no clock time for a date-only event. For a continuing list, use the next step.

Sous anglè: Step 3 — Open a calendar subscription

Sous anglè: Open Reyinyon. Enter a topic in Chèche. Open Plis filtè to choose Peryòd dat or Zòn ki afekte. Then choose Abòne ak kalandriye a in the toolbar above the records. The control appears only when the list contains dated items. If absent, widen the filters until dated records appear. The panel names the list you are about to subscribe to.

Sous anglè: If the filter panel covers the toolbar, close Plis filtè and choose Abòne ak kalandriye a.

Sous anglè: Step 4 — Add the subscription in your calendar app

Sous anglè: In that panel, choose the route your app supports:

  • Sous anglè: Louvri abònman kalandriye a opens your device's calendar app. Add the subscription there. In Apple Calendar, set its Auto-refresh frequency.
  • Sous anglè: Kopye URL abònman an copies an address. In Google Calendar, paste it under Other calendars → From URL. In Outlook, use Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

Sous anglè: Check in your calendar app that it lists a subscribed calendar and displays the expected dates. CityScroll cannot confirm that a subscription was added or refreshed. Importing a downloaded file is a one-time copy. Only a URL your calendar keeps fetching is a subscription.

Sous anglè: Subscriptions keep date-only deadlines as all-day entries and mark cancelled events as cancelled. Updates follow your calendar app's refresh schedule. Check the official record for last-minute changes.

Sous anglè: When dates are missing

Sous anglè: If Now lists an action with no published closing date, there is no deadline to export. Open Alèt to watch for updates instead. If a source could not be reached, the answer is unknown: reload before treating the list as empty.
