Źródło angielskie: Trace a notice to the duty behind it

Źródło angielskie: Follow a Sanitation rule notice to its legal duty, source law and publication evidence.

Źródło angielskie: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

Źródło angielskie: Step 1 — Open the notice

Źródło angielskie: Open the Sanitation final rule notice. No account is needed, and the notice and duty pages read without JavaScript. Check the title: DSNY Final Rule re Implementation Dates for the Brooklyn North and Upper Manhattan Commercial Waste Zones. Open Zobacz w Rejestrze Miejskim beside the notice's actions to compare the city's copy, then return.

Źródło angielskie: Step 2 — Follow the duty

Źródło angielskie: On the notice, find Connected mandate. Read Rules filing for this duty, the relation, and New York City Charter § 753(e)(2), the citation. Select the duty text about regulating commercial waste businesses. It opens the Sanitation duty, with its agency, required action and citation.

Źródło angielskie: Step 3 — Check the law

Źródło angielskie: On the duty page, open Source law. The underlying legislation opens at its official source. Compare the citation and required action, then return to the duty page.

Źródło angielskie: Step 4 — Close the loop

Źródło angielskie: Under Publication evidence, find the notice's request number, 20260605008. It links the filing you started from back to this duty. This establishes that the filing was published. A compliance finding would also need to establish whether the filing met the duty. This page makes no such finding.

Źródło angielskie: Step 5 — Try another notice

Źródło angielskie: Open another rule notice from Przepisy, find Connected mandate, then follow its duty text and Source law. If the block is absent, no supported connection is shown. That does not mean no law applies. Check the notice's own official copy instead.

Źródło angielskie: Optional — Watch for later filings

Źródło angielskie: On a notice with Connected mandate, select Watch this mandate beside the connection. Following opens with the duty selected and needs JavaScript switched on. Check Podgląd dopasowań there. Previewing does not subscribe you. Choose a frequency, enter Adres e-mail under Utwórz tę obserwację, and press Utwórz obserwację only if you want email updates. The page's subscribed confirmation is the saved result.
