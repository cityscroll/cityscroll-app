Źródło angielskie: Find and narrow records

Źródło angielskie: Find a topic, narrow the relevant collection, and keep a link to its filters.

Źródło angielskie: Step 1 — Search for your topic

Źródło angielskie: Open Szukaj, type a word such as housing in the search field and press Szukaj. This needs JavaScript switched on, but no account or email address. Results are grouped by kind of record. Read each result's match explanation before choosing it. Counts change as records change.

Źródło angielskie: Step 2 — Open the relevant collection

Źródło angielskie: Open Browse and choose the kind of record you need. Use People + organizations for agencies, boards and vendors, Kadry for appointments and hiring outcomes, or Egzaminy for civil-service applications. The destination's heading confirms the collection.

Źródło angielskie: Step 3 — Narrow the list

Źródło angielskie: For a dated example, open Posiedzenia. In the filters above the results, enter a word in Szukaj. Open Więcej filtrów to choose Zakres dat, Obszar, którego dotyczy and Agencja. The list updates to match. Other collections offer different controls. Controls and what they give you is an optional lookup.

Źródło angielskie: Step 4 — Keep and reopen the view

Źródło angielskie: Copy the full address from your browser's address bar and paste it into a new tab. Check that the collection and selected filters match. The link repeats your query against the records available when opened. It does not freeze today's results.

Źródło angielskie: Step 5 — Check the record's current opportunity

Źródło angielskie: Open a result by its title and read its status, dates and official-source link. For practice, Caseworker, exam 7016 has a zamknięte application window. That historical record teaches the process. It is not an invitation to apply. For a current application, return to Egzaminy and check an open window at its official source.

Źródło angielskie: When results are empty or unknown

Źródło angielskie: Remove a filter or shorten the keyword in the same controls, then search again. A loaded list with no matches describes the records searched, not everything the city has done. If a source could not be reached, its answer is unknown. Reload or open the record's official source before concluding it has no records.
