অনুসন্ধানইংরেজি মূল: Searches the records for your wordsইংরেজি মূল: A results page grouped by kind of record, with your words in the address
ইংরেজি মূল: The result groupsইংরেজি মূল: Splits results into Contracts, People + organizations, Land, Rules, Meetings and Exams, each with its own countইংরেজি মূল: The kind of record is visible before you open anything
ইংরেজি মূল: Why it matchedইংরেজি মূল: Marks each result as a title match, a summary match, or a match in the body of the recordইংরেজি মূল: For a body match, the passage itself, quoted from the record
পরিসরইংরেজি মূল: Limits a search to one kind of record instead of all of themইংরেজি মূল: A narrower search, kept in the address
সাম্প্রতিক অনুসন্ধানইংরেজি মূল: Lists what you have searched for, with a way to clear itইংরেজি মূল: A list kept in this browser only
ইংরেজি মূল: Browseইংরেজি মূল: Opens one kind of record on its own page, without searchingইংরেজি মূল: A listing you can filter
ইংরেজি মূল: Filters and More filtersইংরেজি মূল: Narrow a listing by the things that listing has — a keyword, an agency, a place, a stage, a date window, an amountইংরেজি মূল: A narrower listing, kept in the address
এই অনুসন্ধান নজরে রাখুনইংরেজি মূল: Takes you to Following with the watch builder openইংরেজি মূল: Nothing is started. The watch is still yours to preview and confirm
ইংরেজি মূল: Export CSV, Export Excel and Printইংরেজি মূল: Take the listing you are looking at with youইংরেজি মূল: A file or a printable page of the listing as filtered

ইংরেজি মূল: Which filters a listing offers depends on what that kind of record has: an amount belongs to contracts, a stage to meetings. The listing itself is the authority on its own filters.

ইংরেজি মূল: Search and the browse listings assemble results in your browser and need JavaScript. Individual record pages, and every page of this guide, do not.

ইংরেজি মূল: Following a topic

নিয়ন্ত্রণইংরেজি মূল: What it doesইংরেজি মূল: What you are left with
অনুসরণ তৈরি করুনইংরেজি মূল: The tab where a watch is built, from a topic and a placeইংরেজি মূল: Nothing is started. Choosing a topic or a place does not start a watch
আরও বিষয়ইংরেজি মূল: Opens the rest of the topics, including staffing and exams, mandates, and a weekly City Council District watchইংরেজি মূল: The same unstarted watch, on a different topic
ফলাফল সীমিত করুনইংরেজি মূল: Adds a keyword, an agency, a City Council District, or a Community Boardইংরেজি মূল: A narrower watch, still unstarted
আরও নির্ভুলভাবে মিলানইংরেজি মূল: On contracts and public meetings, include any or all of a few words, treat an entry as an exact phrase, or exclude words. Exact Council-matter watches and other topics do not show this controlইংরেজি মূল: The same unstarted watch, now matching those words in the title or body of each record
কমিউনিটি বোর্ডের অনুসরণ বেছে নিনইংরেজি মূল: Takes a borough and then a board number, in that orderইংরেজি মূল: A watch identified by both, because a board number alone names several boards
মিল পাওয়া ফলাফলের পূর্বরূপইংরেজি মূল: Shows the records this watch would match, with a summary of what the watch isইংরেজি মূল: Nothing is saved and no email is sent. A preview is not a subscription
ইমেইলের ব্যবধানইংরেজি মূল: Daily when there are matches, or a weekly digest that sends on Mondayইংরেজি মূল: Which of the two you chose
অনুসরণ তৈরি করুনইংরেজি মূল: Starts the watch, using the email address you typeইংরেজি মূল: A running watch. No account is created
আপনার অনুসরণগুলোইংরেজি মূল: Where existing watches are reachedইংরেজি মূল: A note that you open a CityScroll email to get to them, because there is no account to sign in to

ইংরেজি মূল: Nothing on CityScroll subscribes you to anything as a side effect of reading. Starting a watch is always this last control, with your own email address typed into it.

ইংরেজি মূল: Dates and calendars

নিয়ন্ত্রণইংরেজি মূল: What it doesইংরেজি মূল: What you are left with
এর মধ্যে পদক্ষেপ নিনইংরেজি মূল: Separates deadlines you can still act on from the events that follow themইংরেজি মূল: A reading of which dates are yours to meet
ক্যালেন্ডারে সাবস্ক্রাইব করুনইংরেজি মূল: Offers a subscription for what you are looking atইংরেজি মূল: A panel with two ways to take it into your own calendar
ক্যালেন্ডার সাবস্ক্রিপশন খুলুনইংরেজি মূল: Hands the feed to the calendar your device hasইংরেজি মূল: Your calendar app takes over
সাবস্ক্রিপশন URL কপি করুনইংরেজি মূল: Copies the address to paste into a calendar that asks for oneইংরেজি মূল: An address on your clipboard

ইংরেজি মূল: Two limits are worth knowing. ক্যালেন্ডারে সাবস্ক্রাইব করুন appears only where what you are looking at has dated events a calendar could hold. On a listing with no such dates it is not offered, so no empty feed is offered. And once the address has reached your calendar app, CityScroll cannot tell whether your calendar kept the subscription or how often it refreshes — that is a setting in your calendar, and some of them take hours to show a change.

নিয়ন্ত্রণইংরেজি মূল: What it doesইংরেজি মূল: What you are left with

এই সংযোগের লিংক কপি করুনইংরেজি মূল: Copies the address with that one connection already inspectedইংরেজি মূল: An address on your clipboard
লিংক কপি করুনইংরেজি মূল: Copies the address of the record you are onইংরেজি মূল: An address on your clipboard
ইংরেজি মূল: The source linkইংরেজি মূল: Opens the city's own published copy. It is named for where it goes, such as City Record noticeইংরেজি মূল: The city's page, in a new tab
ইংরেজি মূল: Print or save PDF, and Download JSONইংরেজি মূল: Take one record with youইংরেজি মূল: A file of that record as it stands

ইংরেজি মূল: Collecting records

নিয়ন্ত্রণইংরেজি মূল: What it doesইংরেজি মূল: What you are left with
পিন করুনইংরেজি মূল: Adds the record you are on to your investigationইংরেজি মূল: A collection held in this browser only
ইংরেজি মূল: Noteইংরেজি মূল: Adds your own note to a pinned recordইংরেজি মূল: A note held in this browser only
ইংরেজি মূল: .csv রপ্তানি করুন and .json রপ্তানি করুনইংরেজি মূল: Download what you have collectedইংরেজি মূল: A file on your device, each item carrying its link and the date you pinned it
প্রতিবেদন প্রিন্ট করুনইংরেজি মূল: Prepares the collection for printingইংরেজি মূল: A printable page
শেয়ার করুন (শুধু-পঠনযোগ্য লিংক)ইংরেজি মূল: Uploads a read-only snapshot and gives you a link to itইংরেজি মূল: A link anyone you send it to can open, which expires
গবেষণা প্যাকেজ স্থির করুনইংরেজি মূল: Preserves a question and its evidence as a fixed versionইংরেজি মূল: A versioned package that stays as it was, even after newer records arrive
সব মুছে ফেলুনইংরেজি মূল: Empties the investigationইংরেজি মূল: An empty collection. What was in it was on this device

ইংরেজি মূল: In a signed-out visit, pins and notes stay in this browser. Recognized email sessions can keep pins across devices. A shared snapshot includes notes and is readable by anyone with its link for 90 days. Exports are fixed copies. Clearing the local list does not delete exported or shared copies.
