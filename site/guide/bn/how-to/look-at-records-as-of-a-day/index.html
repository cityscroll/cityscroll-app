ইংরেজি মূল: Look at records as of a day

ইংরেজি মূল: Filter an agency to a chosen date and share or clear that view.

ইংরেজি মূল: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 1 — Apply a day

ইংরেজি মূল: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. In the এই দিন পর্যন্ত panel, enter 1 June 2024 in এই তারিখ পর্যন্ত and press প্রয়োগ করুন. The date stays selected and the summary counts records dated on or before it. The Parks example link opens the same filtered view.

ইংরেজি মূল: This filters currently linked records by their event or publication date. It is not a reconstruction of what CityScroll knew that day. Undated records are excluded because they cannot be placed on a timeline.

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 2 — Inspect what was set aside

ইংরেজি মূল: Under the count, open পরবর্তী নথি. These records have dates after your chosen day. Compare the retained and later counts. Their values can change as records are added or corrected. An empty retained set means no dated records currently linked here meet the cutoff, not that the agency did nothing then.

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 3 — Share the selected day

ইংরেজি মূল: While the date is applied, copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that এই তারিখ পর্যন্ত holds the same day and the summary still names the cutoff. Share that address to repeat the filter against the records available when opened.

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 4 — Clear the filter

ইংরেজি মূল: Next to প্রয়োগ করুন, choose মুছুন, which appears after a date is set. The date selection clears and the page again invites you to pick a day. Repeat these steps on another agency from People + organizations when you need a different cutoff.

ইংরেজি মূল: When the filter does not apply

ইংরেজি মূল: Without JavaScript, submitting the date returns an unfiltered page. Enable JavaScript and press প্রয়োগ করুন again, then check the summary before relying on the result.
