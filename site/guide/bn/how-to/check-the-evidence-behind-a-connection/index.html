ইংরেজি মূল: Check the evidence behind a connection

ইংরেজি মূল: Inspect a connection and copy a link that reopens its evidence.

ইংরেজি মূল: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 1 — Open a connection's details

ইংরেজি মূল: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. Under সংযুক্ত নথি, choose বিস্তারিত beside a record's title (announced as বিস্তারিত by a screen reader). A সংযোগের প্রমাণ panel opens for that record.

ইংরেজি মূল: For the worked example, use বিস্তারিত beside Update to Parks List of Qualifying Documents for Disability Membership Fee, if present. The example connection link opens that panel directly when the record is available.

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 2 — Read the basis and source

ইংরেজি মূল: In সংযোগের প্রমাণ, read the match label and date. In the example, প্রকাশিত নথির মাধ্যমে মেলানো হয়েছে and Matched to the agency's published name describe the basis. Under এই সংযোগ কীভাবে স্থাপিত হয়েছে, read the relation, issued rule. Open City Record বিজ্ঞপ্তি in the panel to compare the official source, then return.

ইংরেজি মূল: This supports the stated connection, not a finding of compliance or wrongdoing. How records are connected explains the other match labels.

ইংরেজি মূল: Step 3 — Reopen the evidence from its link

ইংরেজি মূল: At the bottom of the panel, choose এই সংযোগের লিংক কপি করুন. Copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that the same record's সংযোগের প্রমাণ is expanded. Keep this address with the claim it supports.

ইংরেজি মূল: When there is no source or panel

ইংরেজি মূল: Some contract connections have no separate notice source. Open the linked record title in the panel and use that record's own source link. Missing source evidence is not proof of no relationship. If a copied address opens no panel, return to সংযুক্ত নথি and use বিস্তারিত to select a connection currently on the page. Without JavaScript, read the records directly. The panel is unavailable.
