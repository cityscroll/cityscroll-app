ইংরেজি মূল: What a public record tells you

ইংরেজি মূল: What a published record establishes about a government action, and what it deliberately leaves open.

ইংরেজি মূল: Last reviewed 2026-09-06

ইংরেজি মূল: Read what the agency published

ইংরেজি মূল: Almost everything on CityScroll started as something a New York City agency published: a notice in The City Record, a proposed rule, an application filed with the Department of City Planning, an exam announcement. The publication is a fact you can check. The action behind it is a separate thing, and the publication tells you only as much about that action as it was written to tell you.

ইংরেজি মূল: CityScroll is not part of city government and decides nothing. It collects what has been published and links each record to the official source, so the last word is always the city's own.

ইংরেজি মূল: Contracts happen in more than one record

ইংরেজি মূল: A single piece of city buying usually leaves several separate publications behind, and they are not interchangeable.

  • ইংরেজি মূল: A solicitation says the city wants to buy something and invites responses by a date. Nobody has been chosen yet.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: An award notice names who the city intends to give the work to. Registration is a further step that comes after this one.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: A registered contract is the agreement once it has been registered, which is a separate published record from the award.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: A payment is money actually paid against a contract, which arrives later still and in a different record.

ইংরেজি মূল: So a vendor named on an award notice has not necessarily been paid, and a large award figure is not a statement of what was spent. When CityScroll can show more than one of these stages for the same piece of work, it shows them as separate stages rather than merging them into one number.

ইংরেজি মূল: A rule moves through published stages

ইংরেজি মূল: An agency changing its own rules has to publish along the way, and each stage is its own record with its own meaning: a rule is proposed, a public hearing may be held, the comment period closes, the rule is adopted, and it later becomes effective.

ইংরেজি মূল: The gap between those last stages matters. A proposed rule is a proposal, and it can be changed or dropped. A closed comment period means the moment to comment has passed, not that the rule was adopted. And an adopted rule may not be in force yet.

ইংরেজি মূল: Land use is a review, not a single decision

ইংরেজি মূল: A rezoning or other land-use application moves through a review procedure with several bodies in turn — typically a community board, the borough president, the City Planning Commission, and the City Council — and several of them give a recommendation rather than a decision.

ইংরেজি মূল: A record showing a community board voted against an application is a real, meaningful record. It is not the outcome of the application. The official project file at the city's Zoning Application Portal is where the full procedure and its documents live, and the city's own account of the review is the Department of City Planning's public review page.

ইংরেজি মূল: Four different invitations to take part

ইংরেজি মূল: CityScroll surfaces several kinds of opportunity to be heard, and they run under different rules, have different deadlines, and send what you say to different places. They are not one thing.

ইংরেজি মূল: The invitationইংরেজি মূল: What it isইংরেজি মূল: Where what you say goes
ইংরেজি মূল: A public hearingইংরেজি মূল: A scheduled session where people speak, or send written testimonyইংরেজি মূল: The hearing's testimony record. Some bodies publish a transcript, others minutes
ইংরেজি মূল: A rule comment periodইংরেজি মূল: A written window on a proposed agency rule, before a published deadlineইংরেজি মূল: The comment record the agency must consider before adopting
Land-use reviewইংরেজি মূল: Hearings held by the bodies reviewing an application, at their own stagesইংরেজি মূল: That body's recommendation or decision record on the project
ইংরেজি মূল: A contract comment windowইংরেজি মূল: A written window on a proposed contract awardইংরেজি মূল: The public comment record for that procurement

ইংরেজি মূল: Two limits apply to all four. Filing a comment or giving testimony puts your words on the record. No official is obliged to reply to you personally, and CityScroll promises no such reply. And a count of comments stays a count of comments — a body weighs what was said against the law it works under, so comment volume is not a vote and is never reported here as support or opposition.

ইংরেজি মূল: Each listing on CityScroll states which of these applies to it, alongside its official source, and the meetings listing carries a জনমত কীভাবে কাজ করে panel making the same distinctions beside the live records. The city's own pages are the authority on how to take part: testifying at a Council hearing, the rulemaking portal, and hearings on proposed contracts.

ইংরেজি মূল: Boards and districts are different objects

ইংরেজি মূল: Several New York City geographies overlap and share numbers, which makes them easy to confuse.

ইংরেজি মূল: The objectইংরেজি মূল: What it isইংরেজি মূল: Why the difference matters
কমিউনিটি বোর্ডইংরেজি মূল: An appointed local board, numbered within a boroughইংরেজি মূল: Manhattan Community Board 7 and Brooklyn Community Board 7 are different boards
কমিউনিটি জেলাইংরেজি মূল: The area a Community Board coversইংরেজি মূল: It is a place. The board is a body
সিটি কাউন্সিল জেলাইংরেজি মূল: The area one Council Member is elected to representইংরেজি মূল: Numbered citywide, and its boundaries do not follow community districts

ইংরেজি মূল: That is why choosing a Community Board on CityScroll takes two steps — a borough, then a number — while a City Council District takes one. A board number on its own does not identify a board.

ইংরেজি মূল: What a record does not tell you

  • ইংরেজি মূল: A blank is not a zero. Nothing published means nothing was found in the sources CityScroll reads, which is a different claim from nothing happening.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: A closed window is not an invitation. A comment period or application window whose date has passed still teaches you how the process works, but you cannot act on it.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: An estimate is not a deadline. Where CityScroll shows how long something usually takes, that is drawn from past records and is never an official date.
  • ইংরেজি মূল: No published outcome is not a decision against. Many proceedings publish an outcome long after the event, and some never publish one in a form that can be joined to the record you are reading.

ইংরেজি মূল: When you need certainty rather than orientation, open the official source on the record and read the city's own copy. That is what the source link is there for.
