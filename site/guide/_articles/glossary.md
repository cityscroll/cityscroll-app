## How to use this page

These are the terms you meet in the records and in this guide. They come from the journeys the guide
teaches rather than from city government at large. Where a term has an exact meaning somewhere else
on the site, this page gives the plain sense and points at the page that owns the detail.

## Records and publications

| Term | What it means | Where you meet it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The City Record | The city's own daily publication of official notices, and the source behind much of this site | The Official source link on a notice |
| Notice | One published item — a solicitation, an award, a hearing announcement, a rule proposal | Everywhere records are listed |
| Solicitation | A published invitation to respond to something the city wants to buy | Contracts |
| Award | A published statement of who the city intends to give the work to | Contracts |
| Registration | The step after an award that puts a contract in force, published as its own record | The contract lifecycle on a notice |
| Rule | A change to how an agency does something, which must be published before it is adopted | Rules |
| Mandate | A duty an agency has under a law — to report, to make a rule, to hold something — which its publications are evidence about | Connected mandate on a notice |
| Matter | One thing being decided, which several records can belong to | Meetings and contracts |
| Land-use project | An application to change what may be built or how land is used, moving through a published review | Land |
| Exam | A civil-service examination, with its own filing window, conditions and later eligible list | Exams |
| Eligible list | The ranked list of people who passed an exam, from which agencies hire | Exams |

## Bodies and geographies

| Term | What it means | Why it is easy to confuse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency | A city department or office that publishes and acts | Agencies are renamed; a renamed agency keeps one page here |
| Community Board | An appointed local board, numbered inside a borough | Board 7 exists in more than one borough, so a number alone is not a board |
| Community district | The area a Community Board covers | It is a place; the board is a body |
| City Council District | The area one Council Member represents, numbered citywide | Its lines do not follow community district lines |
| Borough president | The elected office for one of the five boroughs, which reviews land-use applications | Its recommendation is not the decision |
| City Planning Commission | The body that reviews land-use applications before the City Council | One stage of a review, not the end of it |
| Vendor | An organization named on a procurement record | The published name is used exactly; spellings are not merged |

## Connections and evidence

| Term | What it means | Where you meet it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connection | A claim that two published records refer to the same thing | Between any two linked records |
| How this connection was made | The panel giving the basis for a connection, its relation and its official source | On a connected record |
| Matched by a published record | Joined on an identifier the publisher issued — the strongest basis | In that panel |
| Record-linkage match | Joined on content such as a name rather than an identifier | In that panel |
| Person-accepted | A pairing someone reviewed and accepted | In that panel |
| Official source | The link to the city's own published copy of the record | On every record |
| Claim link | The address of a page with one connection already inspected, which can be shared | Your browser's address bar |

## Dates and gaps

| Term | What it means | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official date | The date the record is about — a hearing, a deadline, an effective date | Not the publication date |
| Publication date | When the city put the record out | Not the date of the event |
| As of day | A filter keeping only records dated on or before a day you choose | Not a snapshot of what this site knew then |
| Data vintage | How current the underlying source is | Not a promise nothing has been published since |
| Last reviewed | When an editor last checked a guide article against the live site | Nothing about the records |
| Estimate | A range drawn from comparable past records, always labelled | Never an official deadline |
| Not published | No public, joinable release of this field is known | Not a statement that nothing happened |
| Not shown here yet | The city publishes this and CityScroll has not joined it | Not a gap in the city's publishing |

## Identifiers

| Identifier | What it identifies | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PIN | One procurement, which several contract records can share | Published on City Record procurement notices |
| Request ID | One City Record notice | The City Record |
| BBL | One tax lot — borough, block and lot together | City property and tax records |
| Exam number | One civil-service examination | The Department of Citywide Administrative Services |
| Project ID | One land-use application, such as 2022M0258 | The Zoning Application Portal |
| Mandate ID | One duty, such as 64116-001 | An address on this site. The duty itself carries the law it comes from, cited on its page |
