النص الإنجليزي: Follow a search and manage your updates

النص الإنجليزي: Preview a watch, create it with your email address, and manage or stop its updates.

النص الإنجليزي: Last reviewed 2026-09-10

النص الإنجليزي: Step 1 — Set the topic and place

النص الإنجليزي: Open التنبيهات with JavaScript switched on. Under ما الذي تريد متابعته؟, choose a topic such as Contracts and RFPs. Under Where?, choose a borough or Any place. Open تضييق النتائج to add a keyword or agency. For contracts, open طابق بدقة أكبر to include any or all of a few words, treat an entry as an exact phrase, or exclude words. Example: include software, exclude maintenance, preview the awards, then tighten the exclusion if a useful record such as a Greenway project was removed because its body mentioned maintenance vehicles. Frozen award examples are published awards, not currently open bids. The selected choices describe an unsaved watch. No account or password is needed. This is separate from the city's own account system.

النص الإنجليزي: Public meetings use the same طابق بدقة أكبر control. The board, place, and upcoming-event window you already chose stay the boundary. Extra wording only narrows it. Exact Council-matter watches and other topics do not offer these controls.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 2 — Check the preview

النص الإنجليزي: Press معاينة النتائج المطابقة below the choices. Read the rule sentence in ملخص المتابعة, then check معايير المتابعة and the records under معاينة. A preview is not a subscription: nothing is saved and no email is sent. Change a choice and press تحديث النتائج المطابقة to revise it.

النص الإنجليزي: The message No matches now — still watch for new means the loaded preview is empty. You can save a watch for future records, or widen a criterion and preview again. If the preview is not ready, use its retry control. The current matches are unknown.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 3 — Create the watch

النص الإنجليزي: Under وتيرة الرسائل الإلكترونية, choose يوميًا (on days with matches) or أسبوعيًا (Monday digest). Under إنشاء هذه المتابعة, enter عنوان البريد الإلكتروني and press إنشاء متابعة. The watch exists as soon as the request succeeds. There is no confirmation email to click.

النص الإنجليزي: Check for تم اشتراكك — سنرسل إليك رسائل إلكترونية. يمكنك إدارة الاشتراك أو إلغاؤه في أي وقت. Keep the welcome email's manage link. If the page reports an error, use its retry instruction. An error does not confirm enrollment.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 4 — Change or stop updates

النص الإنجليزي: Open the manage link in a CityScroll email. On the watch's row, change the frequency and press Save, or choose Pause, Unpause, or Delete watch. Check the row's resulting state. Edits apply to the next digest, around 9am Eastern. Unsubscribe all watches stops all updates immediately, so use it only when that is what you want.

النص الإنجليزي: When saved watches are unavailable

النص الإنجليزي: Under متابعاتك, an unrecognized browser asks you to open a CityScroll email. Use that email's manage link. A link reported as invalid or expired needs replacing with the link in a recent email. If saved watches cannot load, use the offered retry. That failure does not mean they were deleted. A watch link reported as unrecognized has saved nothing: choose a topic on Following and start again.
