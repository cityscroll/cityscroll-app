النص الإنجليزي: Filter an agency to a chosen date and share or clear that view.

النص الإنجليزي: Last reviewed 2026-09-08

النص الإنجليزي: Step 1 — Apply a day

النص الإنجليزي: Open Parks and Recreation with JavaScript switched on. No account is needed. In the حتى تاريخ panel, enter 1 June 2024 in حتى تاريخ and press تطبيق. The date stays selected and the summary counts records dated on or before it. The Parks example link opens the same filtered view.

النص الإنجليزي: This filters currently linked records by their event or publication date. It is not a reconstruction of what CityScroll knew that day. Undated records are excluded because they cannot be placed on a timeline.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 2 — Inspect what was set aside

النص الإنجليزي: Under the count, open السجلات اللاحقة. These records have dates after your chosen day. Compare the retained and later counts. Their values can change as records are added or corrected. An empty retained set means no dated records currently linked here meet the cutoff, not that the agency did nothing then.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 3 — Share the selected day

النص الإنجليزي: While the date is applied, copy the full browser address and paste it into a new tab. Check that حتى تاريخ holds the same day and the summary still names the cutoff. Share that address to repeat the filter against the records available when opened.

النص الإنجليزي: Step 4 — Clear the filter

النص الإنجليزي: Next to تطبيق, choose مسح, which appears after a date is set. The date selection clears and the page again invites you to pick a day. Repeat these steps on another agency from People + organizations when you need a different cutoff.

النص الإنجليزي: When the filter does not apply

النص الإنجليزي: Without JavaScript, submitting the date returns an unfiltered page. Enable JavaScript and press تطبيق again, then check the summary before relying on the result.
