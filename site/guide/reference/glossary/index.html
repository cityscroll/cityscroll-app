Glossary

The words you meet while reading records on CityScroll, in the sense the city and this site use them.

What does this term mean?

Last reviewed 2026-09-06

How to use this page

These are the terms you meet in the records and in this guide. They come from the journeys the guide teaches rather than from city government at large. Where a term has an exact meaning somewhere else on the site, this page gives the plain sense and points at the page that owns the detail.

Records and publications

TermWhat it meansWhere you meet it
The City RecordThe city's own daily publication of official notices, and the source behind much of this siteThe Official source link on a notice
NoticeOne published item — a solicitation, an award, a hearing announcement, a rule proposalEverywhere records are listed
SolicitationA published invitation to respond to something the city wants to buyContracts
AwardA published statement of who the city intends to give the work toContracts
RegistrationThe step after an award that puts a contract in force, published as its own recordThe contract lifecycle on a notice
RuleA change to how an agency does something, which must be published before it is adoptedRules
MandateA duty an agency has under a law — to report, to make a rule, to hold something — which its publications are evidence aboutConnected mandate on a notice
MatterOne thing being decided, which several records can belong toMeetings and contracts
Land-use projectAn application to change what may be built or how land is used, moving through a published reviewLand
ExamA civil-service examination, with its own filing window, conditions and later eligible listExams
Eligible listThe ranked list of people who passed an exam, from which agencies hireExams

Bodies and geographies

TermWhat it meansWhy it is easy to confuse
AgencyA city department or office that publishes and actsAgencies are renamed; a renamed agency keeps one page here
Community BoardAn appointed local board, numbered inside a boroughBoard 7 exists in more than one borough, so a number alone is not a board
Community districtThe area a Community Board coversIt is a place; the board is a body
City Council DistrictThe area one Council Member represents, numbered citywideIts lines do not follow community district lines
Borough presidentThe elected office for one of the five boroughs, which reviews land-use applicationsIts recommendation is not the decision
City Planning CommissionThe body that reviews land-use applications before the City CouncilOne stage of a review, not the end of it
VendorAn organization named on a procurement recordThe published name is used exactly; spellings are not merged

Connections and evidence

TermWhat it meansWhere you meet it
ConnectionA claim that two published records refer to the same thingBetween any two linked records
How this connection was madeThe panel giving the basis for a connection, its relation and its official sourceOn a connected record
Matched by a published recordJoined on an identifier the publisher issued — the strongest basisIn that panel
Record-linkage matchJoined on content such as a name rather than an identifierIn that panel
Person-acceptedA pairing someone reviewed and acceptedIn that panel
Official sourceThe link to the city's own published copy of the recordOn every record
Claim linkThe address of a page with one connection already inspected, which can be sharedYour browser's address bar

Dates and gaps

TermWhat it meansWhat it is not
Official dateThe date the record is about — a hearing, a deadline, an effective dateNot the publication date
Publication dateWhen the city put the record outNot the date of the event
As of dayA filter keeping only records dated on or before a day you chooseNot a snapshot of what this site knew then
Data vintageHow current the underlying source isNot a promise nothing has been published since
Last reviewedWhen an editor last checked a guide article against the live siteNothing about the records
EstimateA range drawn from comparable past records, always labelledNever an official deadline
Not publishedNo public, joinable release of this field is knownNot a statement that nothing happened
Not shown here yetThe city publishes this and CityScroll has not joined itNot a gap in the city's publishing
