New to civil-service exams? How it works

Civil-service exams can open a path to stable public-service work. You do not need to know City hiring jargon first: start with work that interests you, check the official requirements, and apply during the listed window.

What a civil-service exam does

Find an examOpen-competitive exams are available to anyone who meets the requirements.
Read the NOEThe Notice of Examination is the official guide to the job, qualifications, fee, and test.
Apply in the windowCreate an OASys account, submit the application, and request a fee waiver if you qualify.
Join the eligible listIf you pass, your score places you on a list agencies use when they hire for that title.

Find an exam you can act on

Open exams come first, sorted by the soonest application deadline. Upcoming dates are tentative until DCAS posts the NOE. Choose an interest area — never a personal profile.

Public hearings and meetings

Scheduled, agenda, held, outcomes

City Record hearing notices follow a meeting arc — scheduled, agenda materials, held, then outcomes — with next steps when the notice publishes how to attend or testify. Same-board same-day notices collapse into one card. Affected place still drives near-me. Venue is shown separately.
