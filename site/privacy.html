About CityScroll

Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy covers CityScroll.org. It explains what information we handle and the choices you have when you use the site.

Public records

CityScroll indexes, connects, analyzes and republishes records from government and other public sources. These records can include names, business details and other information the source published. We use public records to build and improve civic tools and research. The source guide explains what is covered and helps you find the original records.

Visiting and searching

We process requests, search terms, browser information, and cookies or local settings to run the site, remember your choices, diagnose problems, and understand how the site is used. We count some actions in aggregate. Completed searches can leave a diagnostic record tied to a browser cookie for up to 30 days. When you’re signed in, recent searches may stay in your account for up to 90 days.

Some pages use Microsoft Clarity to understand clicks, scrolling and other interactions. CityScroll skips loading Clarity when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Other analytics and functional storage can still operate. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement (opens in new tab).

Signing in, following and contacting us

We use email addresses and other information you give us to manage access, send requested updates, respond to messages, investigate reports and improve the site. If you include an optional email address in the feedback form, we use that address to reply. Feedback and technical details, including your IP address and browser information, can stay in our archives and backups for handling reports and preventing abuse.

Service providers and disclosure

Hosting, security, analytics, email and AI providers process information needed for those services. For example, plain-language search may send your words to Anthropic to turn them into filters. You can use the structured search controls to avoid that step. We may publish aggregate usage counts and disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Your choices and questions

Use Following or the link in an email to manage or stop updates. Use your browser settings to manage cookies and stored preferences. Email feedback@cityscroll.org for privacy questions, requests or corrections. For a question about a public record, include the record link.

We post updates here, date them and provide additional notice or choices when required.
